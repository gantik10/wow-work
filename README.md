# Wow Work

**[heywow.work](https://heywow.work)** — the free Wispr Flow alternative for Mac.

Talk instead of typing, take healthy breaks, and keep your desktop clean — all from one small dock on the edge of your Mac's screen.

- **Dictation** — hold **⌃ Ctrl** anywhere and speak; release and clean text appears where your cursor is. Double-tap for hands-free. English, Russian, Ukrainian (and mixes). AI removes "um / ээ / ну" and applies your corrections.
- **Break timer** — 30 minutes of focus, then a short break to stand up and rest your eyes. Pauses when you step away.
- **Main task** — the one thing that matters today, gently reminded until it's done.
- **Organize** — AI tidies a folder into clean sub-folders and suggests what to delete (to the Trash, always undoable). Line up desktop icons in tidy grids — or a smile 😊, heart, circle, wave or spiral.
- **History** — every dictation, one click to copy.

## Install

**Fastest** — open Terminal and paste (installs in seconds, no security prompts):

```
curl -fsSL https://heywow.work/install | bash
```

**Or** [download the .dmg](https://heywow.work/download), drag **Wow Work** onto **Applications**, and on first launch go to
**System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** (once).

Then allow the microphone and Accessibility — Wow Work connects to its free AI by itself (about 5 hours of dictation a month, no account).
macOS 14 or newer, Apple silicon or Intel.

Updates arrive automatically.
